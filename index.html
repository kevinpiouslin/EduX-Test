The idea

Games in the classroom

Gamification is using games to engage kids and get them to do better. It takes the things that make a game hard to put down and puts them inside a lesson.

  • 1

    Points

    Work earns a score, so effort becomes visible instead of invisible.

  • 2

    Levels

    Small steps that get harder, so everyone can see they are moving up.

  • 3

    Challenges

    A puzzle to solve or a record to beat, instead of a page of sums.

  • 4

    Rewards

    A reason to come back tomorrow, and a reason to try again today.

Why it matters

They already want to play

As a teacher, I know students would benefit from this, because they are more inclined to do more work. They like games, so if we can merge the two together, it's perfect.
